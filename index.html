Nocto IPTV Player

Support

Nocto is an IPTV player for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. It plays streams from sources you add yourself (Xtream Codes accounts or M3U playlists). Nocto does not provide, sell or include any channels or content.

Contact

Questions, bugs or feature requests: open an issue. We usually reply within two working days.

Getting started

  1. Open Nocto and choose Add Source.
  2. Enter the server, username and password from your IPTV provider (Xtream Codes), or paste an M3U playlist URL.
  3. Channels, movies, series and the TV guide load automatically. Your sources sync to your other devices through iCloud.

Want to try the app without a provider? Add this M3U playlist of Apple's public test streams: https://michealmouner.github.io/nocto-site/demo.m3u

Frequently asked questions

A channel plays sound but no picture. Open Settings › your source › Player and choose VLC or MPV. Nocto also switches automatically when it detects this.

The guide is shifted by a few hours. Settings › your source › Guide time offset.

How do I share Nocto Pro with my family? Nocto Pro supports Family Sharing. Members of your Apple Family get it automatically on their own devices.

How do I cancel? iPhone/iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Nocto. Apple TV: Settings › Users and Accounts › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings › Subscription › Restore Purchases in Nocto.
