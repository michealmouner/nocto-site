Nocto IPTV Player

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Short version: we don't collect any data about you. Nocto has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We have no servers that receive your information. Besides your IPTV provider and Apple, Nocto only talks to Wikimedia for actor photos (you can turn that off) and, only if you connect your own account, to Trakt.

What stays on your devices and in your iCloud

  • Your sources (server addresses, usernames, playlist URLs), favorites, watch history, playback positions and parental-control settings are stored on your device and synced through your own private iCloud database. We cannot read it.
  • Passwords for your sources are stored in the Apple Keychain (iCloud Keychain if enabled).
  • iCloud Backups you create in Settings are stored in your private iCloud database, with source passwords in an encrypted field.
  • Channel lists, TV guide data and your provider's account details (subscription end date, connections in use) are downloaded from your provider and kept in a local cache on the device.
  • Home Screen widget and Apple TV Top Shelf: to show Continue Watching and Favorites there, Nocto saves their titles and artwork in a folder shared only with its own widget and Top Shelf extension, on the same device.
  • Programme reminders you set in the TV guide are scheduled as local notifications on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is sent to us or to a push service.
  • Actor photos and the results of looking them up are cached on the device for up to a week, and cleared when you refresh a source.

Who Nocto talks to

  • The IPTV providers you add. Nocto connects to them to load channels, guides and streams. Their own privacy policies apply to that traffic.
  • Apple, for iCloud sync and for purchases. Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us if you opt in to sharing with app developers in your device settings. If you use Siri or Shortcuts with Nocto (for example "Play a channel in Nocto"), Apple handles the request under its own privacy policy.
  • Wikimedia (Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons), for cast photos on movie and series pages. When you open such a page, Nocto sends the actor names and the title's TMDB id (if your provider supplies one) to Wikimedia to find freely licensed photos, then downloads those photos. Like any website, Wikimedia sees your device's IP address. No account, identifier or viewing history is sent. You can turn this off in Settings › Actor photos. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.
  • Trakt (optional, off unless you connect it). If you connect your own Trakt account in Settings › Trakt, Nocto sends Trakt the movies and episodes you watch (title, year, season and episode, and how far you got) so they appear in your Trakt history, and adds or removes titles on your Trakt watchlist when you change Watch Later. Live TV is never sent. You sign in on Trakt's own website with a code; Nocto never sees your Trakt password, and it keeps only the sign-in token, in the Apple Keychain. You can turn each part off, or disconnect, in Settings › Trakt. Trakt's privacy policy applies to what you share with it.

Nocto uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.

Children

Nocto does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Parents can use the parental PIN to hide adult categories.

Deleting your data

Delete sources and history in Nocto's Settings, delete iCloud Backups in Settings › iCloud Backups, or remove all of Nocto's iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Questions: contact us.
