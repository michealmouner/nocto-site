Nocto IPTV Player

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Short version: we don't collect any data about you. Nocto has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We have no servers that receive your information.

What stays on your devices and in your iCloud

  • Your sources (server addresses, usernames, playlist URLs), favorites, watch history, playback positions and parental-control settings are stored on your device and synced through your own private iCloud database. We cannot read it.
  • Passwords for your sources are stored in the Apple Keychain (iCloud Keychain if enabled).
  • iCloud Backups you create in Settings are stored in your private iCloud database, with source passwords in an encrypted field.
  • Channel lists and TV guide data are downloaded from your provider and kept in a local cache on the device.

Who Nocto talks to

  • The IPTV providers you add. Nocto connects to them to load channels, guides and streams. Their own privacy policies apply to that traffic.
  • Apple, for iCloud sync and for purchases. Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us if you opt in to sharing with app developers in your device settings.

Children

Nocto does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Parents can use the parental PIN to hide adult categories.

Deleting your data

Delete sources and history in Nocto's Settings, delete iCloud Backups in Settings › iCloud Backups, or remove all of Nocto's iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Questions: contact us.
